**Short version: clone it, read it, play it, change it, share it. Do not sell it
or build a business on it.**

added since the fork is offered noncommercially, and the inherited parts keep

## What "noncommercial" means here

PolyForm Noncommercial states it in its own words, and they are worth reading
rather than paraphrasing. In practice:

**Yes, without asking.** Clone it. Read it. Run it and play it - by yourself, or
on your own network with people you know. Change it. Publish your changes. Hand it to a friend so they can run their own
copy. Study it, write about it, teach from it, stream yourself playing it, fork
it for your own campaign. Personal study, private entertainment, hobby projects
and amateur pursuits are all named in the licence as permitted purposes, as is
use by schools, charities, public research bodies and government institutions.

**No, ask first.**

- **Selling it**, hosting it as a paid service, or bundling it into a commercial
  product. That is the PolyForm Noncommercial part.
- **Making it public.** An internet-facing instance, hosted service, public
  server or bot that strangers can reach, free or paid. Running it privately is
  fine and always was: your own machine, your household, a LAN, a game with
  people you actually know. The line is PUBLIC AVAILABILITY, not the number of
  players. Four friends on your network is fine; one stranger off the internet
  is not.
- **Taking the credit.** If you publish, deploy, demonstrate or write about it,
  credit this project and its upstream by name and link, where people will see
  it. Do not present it as your own creation.

If you want to do any of those, ask. The licence does not forbid the licensor
granting other terms; it only stops you assuming them.

## Why it is not a standard licence, and what that costs

The base is PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 unmodified, plus one added condition,
because no off-the-shelf licence matched. Noncommercial by itself would PERMIT
somebody running a free public instance - that is a noncommercial purpose -
which is the one outcome this project means to prevent. PolyForm Strict blocks
that by blocking redistribution and modification, which is more than is wanted
here. So: standard text, one rider, clearly marked.

The cost of that is real and worth stating. This is no longer plain
`PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`, tooling will report it as a custom licence, and
some people will not touch a custom licence on principle. That is the price of
saying precisely what is meant instead of approximately.

## One honest caveat

The upstream code remains available from upstream under MIT, and nothing here
changes that. If somebody wants the pre-fork engine for a commercial purpose,
they can take it from Mnehmos on MIT terms, and they should. What is licensed
under these terms is this fork and the work in it - which by commit count is the
overwhelming majority of what is here, but is not all of it, and the notice in
`LICENSE-UPSTREAM-MIT` is there because that distinction matters.

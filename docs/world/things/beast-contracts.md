# Beast contracts

A changed beast is a party to an agreement. It is somewhere, travels to meet
somebody, and may refuse terms outside the agreement. Neither a purchase nor
obedience describes the arrangement.

The implementation checklist formerly exported by `beasts.ts` described a gap.
A complete bonded arrangement needs a share of the cultivator's qi draw, the
beast's own continuing progress, a witnessed oath and penalty clause, and
termination when the parties outgrow the terms or lose the ground. The generic
beast actor and oath records exist; automatic draw sharing and termination are
still gaps, not implemented effects of a conversation.

Live questions about the arrangement use `THE_CONTRACT`. Hearing its terms
does not create a bond or change anybody's cultivation rate.

# Moved to [`past-the-ceiling.md`](past-the-ceiling.md)

"Escapes" was the wrong word and it was confusing two different things.

An **escape** in this world is a flight art — the willow step, the reed crossing,
the thing you practise so you can leave a fight you are losing. Several sects
teach two of them and the catalog says so.

This file was about something else entirely: what a cultivator does when their
manual stops carrying them. Nobody is escaping anything there. They have reached
the end of a book and need the next one, and the routes are ways of *acquiring*
it — a later volume, a teacher, a house's shelf, writing one yourself.

The engine module was renamed for the same reason: `escapes.ts` is now
[`acquisition.ts`](../../src/engine/cultivation/acquisition.ts), which is what
its own exports were already called.

This stub exists so links in files under other agents' hands do not break. Delete
it once nothing points here.

# What each dao house teaches

Technique families per domain, keyed to the subjects the houses in
`places-that-teach-a-dao.ts` actually hold. Proposals, not rows - `techniques.ts` is the
catalog and this is the argument for what goes in it.

## Two registers, and keeping them apart is the point

The sects teach 魔功 and 邪功 - Heaven Devouring Art, Blood Demon Scripture, Soul Devouring
Scripture. The dao houses do not talk like that. Their existing arts are clause-named and
bureaucratic - *The Hour That Was Not Taken*, *Footing and Fill Canon* - and a naming pass
today confirmed that is a **deliberate second register** rather than drift.

So the same act has two names depending on who taught it, and that is the texture: a house
of record calls it a lease; a demonic sect calls it devouring. Do not collapse them.

## karma - debt, the lease

The domain the design owner worked out in full, and the one that shows what these are for:
a sufficiently placed cultivator does not attack you. They act on what connects you to
everybody else.

| the act | house register | what it needs that already exists |
|---|---|---|
| harm somebody connected to your enemy | **The Third Party Entry** | the obligation ledger; a grudge already names a holder |
| destroy an enemy's clan as retaliation | **Striking the Whole Roll** | the roster, and the reachability rule in the genre doc |
| follow somebody by the thread, however far they run | **The Debt Does Not Expire** | accounts, which already span years |
| descendants inherit what you did | **Carried to the Next Name** | lineage edges |
| curse everybody connected to somebody | **The Wider Instrument** | closeness (`how-near-you-stand-to-somebody.ts`, unwired) |
| a debt comes due generations later | **Calling the Old Lease** | `accounts-with-no-name.ts`, unwired |
| cut a tie deliberately, and lose the person | **Severance, Filed** | the relationship rows |
| point somebody's divination at the wrong person | **The False Entry** | knowledge and awareness |
| erase your traces so nothing can find you | **Off the Register** | the knowledge gate, inverted |

The nastiest use is the design owner's: kill a man, and years later his son - too weak to
face you - cultivates the wider instrument, and everyone you love starts dying of
accidents. No poison, no assassin, no curse on you. **You are being attacked through the
web of cause and effect that constitutes your life.** And the inverse is as good: a
stranger you saved five hundred years ago has a descendant who became an immortal, and
somebody looks at you and says *I know everyone who matters to you.*

## formation - the diagram, the unlit line

| the act | house register |

| power a diagram with people | **The Unlit Line Lit** |

| read a formation's cost off its shape | **Reckoning the Diagram** |
| leave a line unlit so the whole reads wrong | **One Line Left Unlit** |

**The Unlit Line Lit is the one to build first.** It is the blood-sacrifice formation, and
the design owner's hardest scenario runs through it: a sect's furnace practice powers a
formation protecting three hundred thousand mortals, a reformer shuts the practice down,
and the formation begins to fail. That dilemma is only real if the dependency is real -
practice feeds power feeds formation feeds protection - and none of it can be scripted. It
has to be four systems that actually depend on each other.

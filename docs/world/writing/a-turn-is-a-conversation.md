# A turn is a conversation

What a turn of this game is, and who is on the page for it. Settled by the owner on
2026-09-24, after watching the narrator play on a local model. The narrator's rules that carry
this are in `src/web/prompt.ts` (`whoStaysOnThePage`, `theTurnToWrite`) and
`src/web/the-narrator-plays-the-world.ts` (HOW TO PLAY A TURN).

## What the game is

> the game is dialogues, and between dialogues you move around. but obviously a lot of it is a
> role playing game

Most turns are the player talking to somebody. The rest is getting from one conversation to the
next: travelling, sitting, buying, looking around. The prose is built for the first.

## Who is on the page

**Talking to one person is a one-to-one roleplay.** Only the two of you are on the page. Nobody
else speaks, and nobody else is described.

> if you're talking to an npc, no need to say what the other npcs say, they can just be in the
> background, no need to describe them too

> if i need someone else, the player ought to ask

Somebody else comes onto the page only when the player asks for them, or when the engine puts
them in the turn (a ruling names them), or when a life turns and family is standing there: a
death is not a conversation, and the one who raised you is in the room.

**Talking to the room gets as many answers as the moment needs, never more than three.**

> if i talk to the room, 2-3 people respond, the rest are background noise

> 1 person CAN reply. it depends on what works best for the game. for "who am i" one person
> replying is good, cuz you don't NEED multiple

Three is a ceiling, not a target. The rest of the room is background noise: a line at most,
nobody in it named.

**It switches every turn.**

> and then i talk to 1 person and it turns 1 to 1 again (or talk to the room again)

Looking around, or any turn that is not a conversation, hands the whole room back.

## How somebody enters

> you don't start with their names. think xianxia. you start with a description, like a man in
> xyz (that falls out of their character sheet). he introduces himself as abc

A person enters the prose as the player first sees them, built from their card: what they wear,
their age, what they are at, what their body shows. The name comes after, only as the player
would know it: recognised from home, or given when they introduce themselves.

Family is named by what they are to you:

> you typically say, your grandfather, he ketao, right?

## How it feels

Emotion follows the stakes. Buying food is flat; a parting, a death, a crossing won or lost goes
all the way, the good and the bad alike, and the heavens do not soften it. Talk keeps the
genre's heat either way: quiet turns can exist, but most of the game is talking.

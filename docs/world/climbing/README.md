# climbing

What a cultivator **is**, and what it costs to become more of one. The ladder
itself, the body that climbs it, the arts it climbs with, and what is on the
other side of the top.

**Start here when the question is about a person's own progress** - whether they
can advance, what is stopping them, what a rung means, what a wound does, what
an art or a book is worth.

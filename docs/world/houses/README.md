# houses

Who is out there, what they want, and what happens when you speak to one of them.

**Start here when somebody is in front of the player.** Whether they can be
believed, what their house is, how a person comes to be in one, and what a
question costs to ask.

For everything the repo holds about ONE named house, use
[`../BY-HOUSE.md`](../BY-HOUSE.md) instead - the median house is written about
in fifteen separate files.

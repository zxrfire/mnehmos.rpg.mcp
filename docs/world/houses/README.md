# houses

Who is out there, what they want, and what happens when you speak to one of them.

**Start here when somebody is in front of the player.** Whether they can be
believed, what their house is, how a person comes to be in one, and what a
question costs to ask.

For everything the repo holds about ONE named house, use
[`../BY-HOUSE.md`](../BY-HOUSE.md) instead - the median house is written about
in fifteen separate files.

| File | What it answers |
|---|---|
| [`ancestors.md`](ancestors.md) | ancestral records, the millennial offering, and how prestige decays |
| [`asking.md`](asking.md) | what happens when the player asks anybody anything |
| [`dao-houses.md`](dao-houses.md) | houses that hold a specialisation rather than ground |
| [`discovery.md`](discovery.md) | how anybody comes to know a thing at all |
| [`institutions.md`](institutions.md) | what a sect is as a body, and what it is for from the disciple's side |
| [`offices-and-succession.md`](offices-and-succession.md) | offices, removal, personal disciples, and who succeeds to what |
| [`origin.md`](origin.md) | where a person comes from, and what a birth buys |
| [`patronage.md`](patronage.md) | the pyramid, the feeder relationship, the unbacked, and direct rule |
| [`people.md`](people.md) | the individuals: wants, ties, and what they will do |
| [`sects.md`](sects.md) | the index for the five above - what a house is, split by subject |
| [`standing-powers.md`](standing-powers.md) | the named powers of the region |
| [`trust.md`](trust.md) | being believed - the two axes, the checks, and what each is worth |

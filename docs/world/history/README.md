# history

When things happened, and what the world remembers of them.

**Start here when something old is being explained** - an age that ended, a
decline nobody has noticed, or why a thing that clearly worked once does not now.

| File | What it answers |
|---|---|
| [`ancient.md`](ancient.md) | the ages before this one, and what they left |
| [`the-late-age.md`](the-late-age.md) | the present decline, and who can see it |

## The design is not all in here

**Much of what this repo has decided about the deep past is written in the catalog, not in
the files above**, in header comments and exported constants that no search of
`docs/` reaches. The rows below are routes into it, not summaries of it.

| Catalog | What sends you into it |
|---|---|
| [`history.ts`](../../../src/data/cultivation/history.ts) | an age is invoked, or somebody asks what ended |
| [`lost-ages.ts`](../../../src/data/cultivation/lost-ages.ts) | something survives from an era nobody can reproduce |
| [`catastrophe.ts`](../../../src/data/cultivation/catastrophe.ts) | a disaster is named, and you need what it actually did |
